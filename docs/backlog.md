# Backlog

Work saved for later implementation, after the first engine version. Rules are
in `design.md`; story and adventure rules in `adventure-E01.md`; what the
engine does today in `engine-notes.md`.

## Engine

- [ ] **Burning Luck.** After a roll is shown and before it applies, the player
      may spend Luck for an equal bonus on their own roll. Needs a pause in the
      turn, like confirmations. Warriors and dwarves may also burn 1 Luck to
      cancel a fumble (not needed at 0 level). Design 11.
- [ ] **Real repairs.** An hour to a day or more; needs suitable tools and a
      fitting occupation or a village craftsperson; the item then lasts
      forever. Design 4.7.
- [ ] **Weapon ranges and ammunition.** Use the weapons' close, medium and long
      ranges and Table 4-1 range modifiers; spend ammunition; recover half after
      a won fight (design 11.2).
- [ ] **Reach from the rear rank.** Distinguish reach weapons from missile
      weapons in the rear rank.

## Village (milestone 2)

- [ ] Travel between the village and the dungeon: 3 hours each way, counted
      toward the deadline, no torch time or encounters.
- [ ] Village rest: 8 hours, 1 HP each, once per run. Design 9.5.
- [ ] Village volunteers: four 0-level replacements per run, filling empty
      slots only. Design 8.1a.
- [ ] Move the shop from the dungeon entrance to the village.
- [ ] The priest, the elder and Wren; the trial with its plea; the four endings
      and the village alignment. Adventure sections 5 and 14.
